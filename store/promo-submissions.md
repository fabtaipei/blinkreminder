**Long:**

>
> - Two independent reminders: blink on an interval you choose, look-away on the wall clock (:00 and :30)
> - Click straight through every cue: it never takes focus, never steals a keystroke

> - Every connected monitor, correctly sized, whatever the display scaling
> - 30 languages, following Windows' own display language
> - No account, no ads, no analytics, no network connection of any kind, no camera
>
> Best installed from the Microsoft Store (automatic updates, no SmartScreen prompt). A standalone exe is also available.

## A note for every download site

The standalone exe is **not code-signed**, so Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" on first launch, and it does not update itself. That is why every listing points people to the Microsoft Store first. If a site insists on hosting the file, expect a few "is this safe?" comments; the answer is that the source is public on GitHub.
