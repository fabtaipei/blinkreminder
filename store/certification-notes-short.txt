Tested by the developer on Windows 11 (build 26200), single and dual monitor, on 17 September 2026.

THIS APP HAS NO MAIN WINDOW. It runs in the notification area. After launching, look for a small amber ring next to the clock; you may need the ^ arrow to show hidden icons. Right-click it for: Blink now, Snooze, Settings, Quit.

TO SEE IT WORK IMMEDIATELY: right-click the tray icon and choose "Blink now". Or open Settings and use the "Preview blink" and "Preview break" buttons at the bottom.

EXPECTED BEHAVIOUR, NOT DEFECTS:
- A small dot sits in the corner of each screen. That is the running indicator. It is click-through.
- Reminders pass mouse clicks straight through and never take keyboard focus, by design.
- Reminders do not paint over UAC prompts or exclusive-fullscreen games. The app runs at medium integrity; this is intended.
- A window appears on first launch to say the app is running in the background. Dismiss it.

No network connection is needed to test anything. There is no account, sign-in, trial or in-app purchase.
