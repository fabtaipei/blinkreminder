# Privacy Policy — Dry Eyes Blink Reminder Lite

_Covers the Windows app and the Chrome extension._

_Last updated: 22 October 2026_

## The short version

Dry Eyes Blink Reminder Lite collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no account. It has no
network code of any kind. That is true of both the Windows app and the
Chrome extension.

## What the app stores, and where

Two files, both on your own computer, both in your own user folder:

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `config.json` | Your settings: reminder intervals, colours, sizes, sounds, positions. |
| `activity.log` | A plain-text record of when reminders fired and of any errors, kept so that a problem can be diagnosed. It is capped at 512 KB and older entries are discarded. |

In the version installed from the Microsoft Store these live inside the app's
own per-user data folder, and **Windows deletes them when you uninstall the
app**. In the portable version they live in `%APPDATA%\BlinkReminder` and you
can delete that folder yourself at any time.

Neither file contains anything about you personally. Neither is ever
transmitted anywhere, because the app has no code that transmits anything.

## What the app does not do

- No user accounts, sign-in, or registration
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage statistics
- No advertising and no advertising identifiers
- No network requests — the app never contacts any server, including ours
- No camera or microphone access. Dry Eyes Blink Reminder Lite works on a timer; it does not
  watch you
- No reading of your documents, browsing history, keystrokes, or screen content
- Nothing is shared with, or sold to, anybody

## The two links in the app

Dry Eyes Blink Reminder Lite can open exactly two things in your browser or in Windows
Settings, and only when you click them:

- **Buy me a coffee** — opens `buymeacoffee.com`, a third party with its own
  privacy policy. The app sends nothing with the link; it simply opens the page.
- **Startup settings** — opens Windows' own Startup apps page.

The app refuses to open anything that is not an `https://`, `http://` or
`ms-settings:` address.

## Sounds

Reminder sounds are read from the sound files that ship with Windows, in
`C:\Windows\Media`. Nothing is downloaded and no other file on your computer is
read.

## The Chrome extension

The extension is the same reminder, inside the browser. It behaves the same
way: nothing is collected, nothing is transmitted, and there is no account.

**What it stores.** Your settings, a count of the days you have used it, and
how far through the short tutorial you are. These are kept by the browser on
your own device, through Chrome's own extension storage. They are not synced
and not sent anywhere. Removing the extension removes them.

**Access to web pages.** To draw the cue on a page, Chrome requires the
permission it describes as *"read and change all your data on all websites"*.
That is Chrome's wording for a single permission covering both; there is no
narrower one that permits drawing without reading.

The extension is installed **without** it. It is requested only if you switch
on **Show the cue on web pages**, and you can narrow or withdraw it at any
time from Chrome's own menu. Used, it does exactly one thing: it adds an
element of the extension's own to the page and removes it again. It does not
read the page's text, forms, cookies, addresses or anything you type, and it
could not send them anywhere if it did, because the extension contains no
network code at all.

**What it may show instead.** Without that permission, the look-away reminder
arrives as a browser notification and the blink cue is not shown.

**Sounds.** The chime is generated by the extension as it plays. Nothing is
downloaded and no file on your computer is read.

**Links.** The extension can open the Buy Me a Coffee page, its own store
listing for a review, and the Microsoft Store listing for the Windows
version — each only when you click it, and each sending nothing with it.

## Children

Dry Eyes Blink Reminder Lite is a general-purpose utility. It collects no information from
anyone, including children.
