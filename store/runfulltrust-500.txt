Packaged Win32 desktop app (EntryPoint=Windows.FullTrustApplication, medium integrity), so runFullTrust is mandatory for this app model and is the only capability declared. Used for: click-through overlays via SetWindowLongW with WS_EX_LAYERED|TRANSPARENT|NOACTIVATE, so a reminder never steals focus; EnumDisplayMonitors and GetDpiForMonitor for correct placement on every screen; Shell_NotifyIcon for the tray icon; PlaySound for Windows' own .wav files. No network, camera, elevation or telemetry.
