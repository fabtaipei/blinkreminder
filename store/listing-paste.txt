Dry Eyes Blink Reminder Lite is a small, quiet utility that lives in your system tray and gives you a brief visual cue to blink — and, on a separate timer, to look away from the screen for a moment.

The cue is deliberately subtle. It is a soft pulse, a dot, or a single word, shown for a fraction of a second and then gone. You can click straight through it: it never takes focus, never steals a keystroke, and never gets in the way of what you were doing.

TWO INDEPENDENT REMINDERS

The blink reminder fires on an interval you choose — anything from a few seconds upward. The look-away reminder runs on the wall clock, so it lands at the same minutes past the hour no matter when you started the app. Each has its own completely separate settings: its own style, colour, size, position, timing and sound. Changing one never touches the other.

MADE FOR MORE THAN ONE SCREEN

Reminders appear on every connected monitor, correctly sized on each one even when your displays run at different scaling. A small indicator dot in the corner of each screen shows the app is running, with a countdown to the next break above it.

YOURS TO TUNE

Choose a gentle dim, a coloured dot, or a word of your own. Set the size, position, opacity, fade and hold. Pick from four sounds, each with its own volume, or leave it silent. Preview any change before saving.

QUIET, AND PRIVATE BY CONSTRUCTION

No account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. There is no network connection of any kind — the app never contacts any server, including ours. It does not use your camera: it works on a timer, it does not watch you.

GOOD TO KNOW

Dry Eyes Blink Reminder Lite runs in the notification area and has no main window. Right-click its tray icon for Settings. The reminder pulse is rate-limited in code so that no combination of settings can exceed three flashes per second.
